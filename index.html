OnCourse Query Helper

A quick bookmarklet tool to batch-query multiple courses at once on OnCourse.

OnCourse Query
Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar

How to Use It:

  1. Drag the button above directly into your browser bookmark bar.
  2. Navigate to oncourse.college.
  3. Click the OnCourse Query bookmark from your bar.
  4. Paste your list of course codes into the popup to query them all at once.
why does this exist?

Because I found it tedious to compare courses from long lists of long B.S., M.S., or minor requirements :D
